Ribbly Screwspigot

Bring Ribbly's Head to Yuka Screwspigot in the Burning Steppes.
Ribbly's Head (1)

Description

My brother Ribbly has always been a drain to his family, taking our money and wasting it on one scheme after another.

Well, his last scheme must have been his worst, because Baron Revilgaz of Booty Bay just put a price on poor Ribbly's head. I can't tell you how happy this makes the Screwspigots! Our little Ribbly's finally worth something!

But now he's hiding out, deep in Blackrock Depths. Please, find him and bring him to me!

Or, if he won't come, then that's ok... just bring me his head.

Progress

Do you have Ribbly?  Or, at least a part of him?

Completion

Aha!  You found him!  And... it appears that my little brother didn't want to come quietly.  It's a pity.  I would have liked to see the look on his face when I told him our plans to turn him in to Revilgaz.

Thank you, <name>.  You've made my family very happy.  And Ribbly's never looked as peaceful as he does now.
